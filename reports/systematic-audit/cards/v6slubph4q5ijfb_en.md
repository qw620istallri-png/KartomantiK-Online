# Quasimillenary

Identifiant : `v6slubph4q5ijfb_en`

## Texte de référence

As long as Quasimillenary is in the Interzone, in the Recovery Phase you may choose to shuffle your Limbo and draw cards from it instead of from your deck. Cards drawn from the Limbo this way must remain uncovered in front of you but are considered as cards in hand. If these cards are about to enter the Limbo or the deck or if, after entering the field, they are about to enter a zone that is not an Empathic Vessel, exile them instead.
