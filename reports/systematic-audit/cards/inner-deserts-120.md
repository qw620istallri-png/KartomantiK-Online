# Overwhelming Persuasion

Identifiant : `inner-deserts-120`

## Texte de référence

After the Revelation, a target opponent puts a Will from their hand face up on the field. The chosen Will can be played by that player as if it were in their hand and costs 2 🔘 essences more to play. That player loses 5 points at the end of each turn for each Will that remains on the field due to this effect.
