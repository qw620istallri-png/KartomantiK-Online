# Dol, the Aching

Identifiant : `inner-deserts-061`

## Texte de référence

When Dol enters the field, you may Chain a target Manifestation you own from an Empathic Vessel.
If the Chained Manifestation wins the Confrontation, exile it.
If the Chained Manifestation would leave the Confrontation Zone due to cards or effects, it returns to the Empathic Vessel from which it was moved instead.
