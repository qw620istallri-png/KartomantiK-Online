# Simulacrum of Clemency

Identifiant : `inner-deserts-023`

## Texte de référence

Whenever an opponent plays a Will, you gain 5 Points unless that player decides to neutralize that Will.
If a Will is neutralized this way, Exhaust Simulacrum of Clemency.
As long as Simulacrum of Clemency is Exhausted, it has no effects.
