# Balance of the Spirit

Identifiant : `inner-deserts-137`

## Texte de référence

Whenever the player with the most Points in their Empathic Vessel is about to gain Points, prevent that gain. Then all other players gain an equal amount.
Whenever the player with the fewest Points in their Empathic Vessel is about to lose Points, prevent that loss. Then all other players lose an equal amount.
