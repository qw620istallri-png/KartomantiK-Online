# Sangen, the Relic

Identifiant : `inner-deserts-057`

## Texte de référence

When Sangen wins a confrontation, it enters a target opponent's Interzone under their control.
As long as Sangen is in the Interzone, it cannot be replaced, and all other Manifestations in its controller's Interzone get -1 power.
If at the end of the game Sangen is in the Interzone, its controller gains 25 points.
