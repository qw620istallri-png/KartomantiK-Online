# Alchemical Press

Identifiant : `ze99bj8uw2oisfx_en`

## Texte de référence

The Alchemical Press enters the field with 5 Formula counters on it.
As long as there are Formula counters on the Alchemical Press, you can use the manifestations in your Limbo to pay tributes. If you do, exile that manifestation and remove a Formula counter from the Alchemical Press.
Whenever you play a Will, you can discard a card. If you do, draw a card.
